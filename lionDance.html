About Our Lion Dance Team

The East Hampton Lion Dance Team consists of talented teenagers from the East Hampton neighborhood and dedicated volunteers from Walton High School. Together, this talented group is committed to bringing the vibrant traditions of Chinese culture to our community. The lion dance is a spectacular performance art form that dates back centuries, symbolizing prosperity, good fortune, and joy.

Whether you're interested in learning the art of lion dancing, watching a thrilling performance, or supporting our team, we welcome your participation and enthusiasm!
